2. Google Docs ↔ Gemini Phone App Middleman Bridge

Because the Gemini Mobile App natively reads @Google Drive / @Google Docs, this middleman syncs your PC terminal & workspace brain into a live Google Doc named 9LMNTS_GEMINI_LIVE_BRAIN and polls Line 1 (RUN_CMD: ...) every 10 seconds to run commands from your phone!

⬇ Download Brain (.md) for Drive 📜 View Google Apps Script (.gs)
Ask your Gemini Phone App:
"@Google Drive read 9LMNTS_GEMINI_LIVE_BRAIN and tell me the latest build and terminal output."

Run a Command from your Phone via Google Docs:
Open 9LMNTS_GEMINI_LIVE_BRAIN in Google Docs on your phone and change Line 1 to:
RUN_CMD: build  or  RUN_CMD: read ai-voice.html
